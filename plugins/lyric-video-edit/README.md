# lyric-video-edit

Turns pasted song lyrics into a vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) silent lyric video:

- CAPS caption at the top, words pop in one by one, key words on colored plates.
- Under it, one card per line with a hand-drawn animated app screen that acts out the line (chat, heart monitor, letter, polaroids, calendar, map…).
- Mood presets: default, slow, sad (muted colors, rain, broken hearts), hype.
- Sensitive lines (pills, self-harm, suicide) keep their text but are illustrated neutrally.

Just send lyrics and say "сделай эдит". Add the music yourself afterwards.

Requirements in the workspace: python3, playwright with chromium, ffmpeg, fonts Fira Sans and Lora.
